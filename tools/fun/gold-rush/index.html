What the augments pay

Numbers come from the game files via CommunityDragon: Mayhem's starting gold, passive gold, kill and minion gold, item prices and the augment values. Assist gold is not in the files in a readable form, so it is a best guess you can change. Kills, assists, last hits and augment procs are steady rates you set, starting at 1:00. Each item is done when your gold covers its full price; components and selling are not modeled. Left out: first blood, kill streak bounties, minion gold growth over time, gold from nearby minion deaths and stat anvils. Upgrade augments only pay once you own their item, and a free component from a quest counts toward the next item built from it.
