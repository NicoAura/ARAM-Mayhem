Where the Health comes from

Numbers come from the game files via CommunityDragon, with Mayhem's Heartsteel value (5% of proc damage becomes Health). Health % bonuses (Goliath, Warmog's, Tank Engine and so on) are added together and multiply all max Health, Heartsteel stacks included. A proc needs 3 seconds near an enemy champion and each champion can be procced once every 30 seconds.
