Where the extra damage comes from

Champion stats, item penetration and augment values come from the game files via CommunityDragon, with Mayhem's item changes applied. Resists are cut by % reduction, then % penetration, then flat penetration and Lethality, and damage taken is 100 ÷ (100 + resist). Damage bonuses multiply together. The target builds are plain numbers we picked, not real items. Not counted: ARAM's per-champion damage adjustments, the target's shields, healing and damage reduction, Shadowflame and other effects that need the target already hurt, and Dropkick's stat scaling (type your own threshold).
