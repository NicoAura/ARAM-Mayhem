The mode

ARAM with augments

ARAM: Mayhem is ARAM on the Howling Abyss with Arena-style augments. You pick an augment from a hand of Silver, Gold or Prismatic cards at set levels, and you can reroll the hand. Some augments are quests: hit a goal during the game and they turn into something bigger, including items you can't buy anywhere else. Everything on this page is read from the game client's files.
