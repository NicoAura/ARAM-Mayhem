Source. Tencent's 101.qq.com backend for 海克斯大乱斗, Chinese servers only. Tencent doesn't publish game counts.

How ranges work. Tencent's daily champion numbers are running totals for the current patch. A whole patch shows Tencent's own final figures. Months and custom dates subtract the running totals at the start of the range from those at the end, and estimate each champion's games as pick rate × days. Ranges of a day or two are rougher than whole patches.

Augments and duos. Tencent only serves the current patch window for these, so they don't follow the time range.
